Brightline

An agreement fuzzer, run by the network that will later judge the agreement. Every number below comes from real GenLayer transactions.

Scope. Split Score is an experimental studionet lab instrument. The E6 calibration study failed its matched-pair criterion, so it is not validated for ranking two drafts of the same clause and is not used for production gating. It is not a probability of dispute, not a prediction of how a court would rule, and decidability is not fairness — a rule can be trivial to adjudicate and still be one-sided. The primary output is the counterexample, not the number.
